# pandel.py

Pandel photon-arrival-time distribution, scipy implementation.

Entry at [L11](../../nugget/surrogates/pandel.py#L11). The Pandel PDF
`p(t | d)` (gamma-shaped, distance-dependent) is the standard
analytical form for diffuse Cherenkov photon timing in water.
